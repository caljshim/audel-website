Common questions

How do I get access?

Audel is in a private beta. Sign-up is open only to people who've been invited by email.

Can Audel move my money?

No. Bank connections are read-only. Audel can see balances and transactions; it can't make payments or transfers.

How do I disconnect my bank?

Email us and we'll remove the connection, or delete your account to remove every connection at once.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open your profile, tap Account & sign-in, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your data and disconnects your banks. You can also email us to ask for deletion.
