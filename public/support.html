Common questions

How does the free month work?

Every new account gets 30 days free, with no card needed. The app shows how many days are left. After that, Audel is $4.99 a month if you choose to subscribe; if you don't, the app shows a subscribe screen and your data is kept.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Audel, and tap Cancel Subscription. Or in Audel, open your profile, tap Account & sign-in, then Manage subscription. You keep Audel until the end of the month you've paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Subscriptions are paid to Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you think you were charged by mistake, email us and we'll help.

I don't recognize a charge.

Audel subscriptions appear on your statement as a charge from Apple (APPLE.COM/BILL). You can see every subscription on your Apple Account in Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.

Can Audel move my money?

No. Bank connections are read-only. Audel can see balances and transactions; it can't make payments or transfers.

How do I disconnect my bank?

In the app, open your profile, tap Connected accounts, then press and hold one of that bank's accounts and tap Disconnect. Audel removes the connection at Plaid and deletes that bank's accounts and transactions. Deleting your account removes every connection at once.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open your profile, tap Account & sign-in, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your data and disconnects your banks. It doesn't cancel an App Store subscription: cancel that in your iPhone's Settings (tap your name, then Subscriptions). You can also email us to ask for deletion.
