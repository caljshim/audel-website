All your accounts in one list

Transactions from every account land in Finances as they clear. Home turns them into one number: what you can still spend this month.

2

Asking for a budget

Audel reads the balances and the bills already coming, works out what the goal needs each month, and lowers the budgets that need to come down.

Typed into Ask Audel, from inside Finances.

Keeping the budget current

Each purchase is filed as it clears and its budget updates with it, so the amount left to spend stays accurate through the month.

Ask Audel anything

1

Today's plan under the week

Events, reminders and routines in one list. A routine is a checklist on the weekdays you choose, and the week's routines share one bar.

2

Adding events from a photo

Photograph a paper planner or a flyer and Audel adds the events it can read. If a line is unreadable, Audel says so and leaves it out.

Shown a photo: hold the button, then Camera.

3

Reminders that ring through Focus

Mark a reminder important and it rings like an alarm, through silent and Focus. Answer it from the notification: Done, Snooze or Stop.

Repeat until it's doneKeep reminding me until I stop it, every 30 minutes to every 4 hours.

Connectors

Bring the apps you already use

Connect Apple Health once, and a goal can read from any app that writes to it. Pick the source when you set the goal up: runs from Strava, sleep from Oura, protein from MyFitnessPal.

Apple HealthOne connection, read-only. Audel never writes back.
  • S Strava Runs, rides and distance Fitness
  • F Fitbit Steps, active minutes, sleep Fitness
  • G Garmin Connect Workouts, distance, pace Fitness
  • P Peloton Classes and output Fitness
  • N Nike Run Club Runs and weekly mileage Fitness
  • O Oura Sleep, readiness, HRV Sleep and recovery
  • W WHOOP Recovery, strain, sleep Sleep and recovery
  • M MyFitnessPal Calories and macros Food
  • C Cronometer Protein, fibre, micronutrients Food
  • W Withings Weight and body composition Body
  • C Calm Mindful minutes Mind
  • H Headspace Meditation streaks Mind

Any app that writes to Apple Health works, not only these. Anything else can still be logged by telling Audel.
