Arbitration, in plain words

A short guide to one section of the Terms of Service. The Terms are what apply; this page only explains them.

What arbitration is

Arbitration is a way to settle a disagreement without going to court. A neutral arbitrator listens to both sides and makes a decision, instead of a judge or jury. It's usually quicker and less formal than a court case.

How it applies to Audel

If you and we ever have a dispute we can't settle by talking it through, the Terms say it goes to individual arbitration with the American Arbitration Association, under its consumer rules. Any hearing is in the county where you live or by video, whichever you prefer. Small claims court is still available for claims that qualify.

What opting out changes

If you opt out, disputes between you and us are resolved in court instead of arbitration. Nothing else about Audel or the rest of the Terms changes: your account, your data and your subscription stay exactly as they are.

How to opt out

You can opt out within 30 days of agreeing to the current Terms, in either of two ways:

  • In the Audel app: open your profile, tap Account & sign-in, and under Legal tap Opt out of arbitration. The row then shows the day you opted out.
  • By email: write to support@audel.ai from the email address on your Audel account, with your name and a sentence saying you opt out of arbitration.

Questions

Write to support@audel.ai. The full wording is in Resolving disputes and Opting out.
