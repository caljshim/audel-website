The subscription and automatic renewal

To keep using Audel after the free month, you can subscribe for $4.99 (US dollars) per month in the US, or the price shown in the App Store where you live, including any tax that applies. You subscribe in the app through Apple's App Store, which shows the price and the renewal terms before you confirm. Apple handles the payment: we never see your card or other payment details. If you subscribe before your free month ends, the subscription starts, and is charged, that day.

Your subscription renews automatically. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews every month, and your Apple Account is charged for the next month within 24 hours before the current month ends, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. It continues month to month with no end date until you cancel. Apple sends a receipt for each charge.

If we ever change the price, you'll be told in advance, by Apple and in the app, at least 30 days before the new price applies to you, and you can cancel before then. A price change never applies to a month you've already paid for.

If a renewal payment fails, Apple tries again over the following days and asks you to update your payment method. If payment still can't be collected, your subscription ends and Audel shows the subscribe screen, as it does when a free month ends.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time, and it's as easy as subscribing:

  • On your iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Audel, and tap Cancel Subscription.
  • In the app: open your profile, tap Account & sign-in, then Manage subscription. This opens Apple's subscription page, where you can cancel.

Because Apple handles the subscription, we can't cancel it for you, but if you email support@audel.ai we'll walk you through it. When you cancel, no further charges are made. You keep full access until the end of the monthly period you've already paid for; after that, Audel shows the subscribe screen. Deleting the Audel app from your phone, or deleting your Audel account, does not cancel your subscription: cancel it in Settings too.

When access ends, your data is kept

If your free month ends without a subscription, or a subscription ends, Audel shows only a subscribe screen. From there you can still subscribe, sign out, read these terms, or delete your account. We don't delete your goals, routines, history, budgets, balances or past transactions when access ends. While your account has no access, we stop refreshing your bank data and stop sending you notifications. If you still haven't subscribed 30 days after access ends, we disconnect your bank connections at Plaid, so Audel no longer has access to your banks; we send a notification 3 days before. Everything Audel already imported is kept. Subscribing again restores the rest at once, and you can reconnect your banks from Connected accounts to bring in new transactions.

We keep an inactive account until you delete it. If we ever decide to remove accounts that have been inactive for a long time, we'll update these terms and tell you by email first.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app (Account & sign-in, then Delete account), including from the subscribe screen, or by emailing us. Deleting your account permanently deletes your data and disconnects your banks. It does not cancel an App Store subscription, which Apple manages: cancel it in your iPhone's Settings (see Cancelling) so you aren't charged again. Deletion can't be undone.

Your bank connections and money

Bank and investment connections are made through Plaid and are read-only: Audel can see balances and transactions, but it can't make payments, transfers or trades. Audel's numbers, budgets and suggestions are there to help you keep track. They aren't financial, tax or legal advice, and they depend on the data your bank provides, which can be late or incomplete. Check anything important with your bank.

You can connect up to 10 banks. You can disconnect a bank at any time in the app: open your profile, tap Connected accounts, press and hold one of that bank's accounts, then tap Disconnect. This removes the connection at Plaid and deletes that bank's accounts and their transactions from Audel.
