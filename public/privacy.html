Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

The short version

  • Audel uses your data to run the app for you. It doesn't sell or share it, show you ads, or track you across other apps.
  • Bank connections are read-only. Audel can't move money.
  • Before the assistant first answers you, the app asks you to agree to your messages, and the data it needs to answer them, going to AI providers to write a reply.
  • We read conversations with the assistant, without your name or email, to find and fix its mistakes. You can turn this off in the app.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

Audel is an iPhone app for tracking goals, routines and personal finances, operated by Caleb Shim ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use Audel, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. Questions go to support@audel.ai.

What we collect

Your account

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address and, from Google, your profile photo. We use these to identify your account and show your profile. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address instead of your real one. If you sign in with Apple, we also keep a token Apple gives us, stored encrypted, so that deleting your account can end Audel's access to your Apple sign-in too.

What you put into Audel

Your goals, routines, reminders, calendar events, check-ins, notes and progress logs, and the times you check things off. Audel uses the times you usually complete a routine to schedule reminders.

Bank and card accounts

If you connect a bank, we use Plaid to read your account names, balances and transactions, and, for investment and retirement accounts, your holdings and investment transactions. Your bank login goes to Plaid and your bank, never to us. Plaid gives us an access token for each connection, which we store encrypted. Access is read-only: Audel can see your accounts but can't make payments, transfers or trades. Plaid's handling of your data is covered by its own privacy policy.

Payments

If you subscribe, you pay Apple through the App Store, with the payment method on your Apple Account. Apple processes the payment; your card details and billing address go to Apple, never to us. Apple tells us the subscription's status: which plan, whether it's active or renewing, and until when, with the IDs Apple gives the purchase. We store those, and a random ID the app attaches to the purchase so it belongs to your Audel account, so the app knows whether it's open to you. Apple's handling of your payment is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Apple Health

If you connect a goal to Apple Health, Audel reads the specific data you allow, such as workouts, steps, sleep or nutrition, and stores the values it needs to track that goal. Audel only reads Health data; it never writes to Apple Health. Health data is used to show your progress and, when you ask the assistant about it, the values it needs to answer are sent to the AI providers that write its replies (see How the assistant works). Those conversations are included in the copies we review to improve Audel, without your name or email, unless you turn that off (see Improving Audel). Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with data brokers.

Location

If you set up a goal that counts visits to a place, such as the gym, your iPhone watches for arrivals at the place you choose. Audel records that a visit happened and when. Your coordinates stay on your device; we don't receive them.

Photos, camera and voice

Photos you attach to a chat with the assistant are uploaded and stored with your account so the assistant can read them. Voice input is transcribed on your iPhone; we receive the text, not the audio.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, your iPhone gives us a push token, which we store with your account and use only to send you Audel's notifications through Apple's push notification service.

Usage needed to run the service

We record how much of the assistant you use, to apply usage limits, and keep server logs for security and troubleshooting. Server logs include technical details such as your IP address, request times, and errors and crash reports the app sends us. Audel does not include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

How the assistant works

The first time you open the assistant, the app tells you what is sent where and asks you to agree. Until you do, the assistant doesn't answer and Audel doesn't send your data to any AI provider. You can take your agreement back at any time under Account & sign-in.

When you message Audel's assistant, your message and the relevant parts of your Audel data (for example, the goals, transactions, balances or Health values it needs to answer) are sent to AI providers to generate a reply. We currently use Google (Gemini) and Anthropic (Claude) through their paid APIs. When the assistant looks something up on the web, the search terms are sent to Brave Search. To suggest which budget a new transaction belongs in, its merchant, amount, date and bank category, and the names of your budgets, are sent to TypeSafe. We keep your chat history so you can see past conversations.

When you look up a packaged food by barcode or name, the barcode or search words are sent from our servers to Open Food Facts. Nothing that identifies you is sent with them.

Improving Audel

To find where the assistant got something wrong and fix it, we review conversations with it. Each night, the previous day's messages are copied from our database to private storage in Google Cloud, with your name, email and account ID left out and replaced by a random label. Photos you attached are never copied, only how many there were. Message text can include whatever the assistant used to answer, such as amounts or Health values. We, and an AI model we use to help with this review (Anthropic's Claude), read these copies to find mistakes, and may turn a mistake into a test case built from made-up example data, never your own. The copies are deleted after 30 days.

This applies only to conversations from October 16, 2026 onward. You can turn it off at any time, including from the screen that first asks about the assistant: open your profile, tap Account & sign-in, and switch off Improve Audel with my conversations. From then on your messages are excluded at the database level, and your chat history is still kept so you can see it.

Who processes your data

We share data only with the service providers that run Audel for us, and only what each needs:

  • Google Cloud, which hosts our servers and database in the United States
  • Plaid, for bank and investment connections
  • Google and Anthropic, for the assistant's replies, and Anthropic to help review conversations (see Improving Audel)
  • Brave, for web searches the assistant makes
  • TypeSafe, for suggesting which budget a transaction belongs in
  • Open Food Facts, for food barcode and name lookups
  • Google and Apple, for signing in, and Apple, for subscription payments and delivering notifications

We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Our database sits on a private network with no public address, and our servers sign in to it without a stored password. Bank access tokens and Apple sign-in tokens are encrypted with a separate key. Each account's data is isolated at the database level, so one user can't read another's.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. If your account goes 30 days without access (no subscription after the free month, or after a subscription ends), we remove its bank connections at Plaid; data already imported is kept for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database immediately, your bank connections are revoked at Plaid, and Audel's access to your Apple sign-in is revoked at Apple. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription, because Apple manages it: cancel it in your iPhone's Settings (tap your name, then Subscriptions) so you aren't charged again. Apple keeps records of your purchases under its own policy. Copies in our encrypted backups expire within 7 days. Server logs are kept for 30 days. Conversation copies made for review are deleted after 30 days.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, open your profile, tap Account & sign-in, then Delete account. Or email us.
  • The assistant and AI providers: switch off Let Audel use its AI providers under Account & sign-in. The assistant stops answering and nothing more is sent to them.
  • Disconnect a bank or Apple Health: in the app, open your profile, tap Connected accounts, press and hold one of the bank's accounts, then tap Disconnect. This removes the connection at Plaid and deletes that bank's accounts and transactions from Audel. To stop Apple Health access, turn it off in the iPhone's Settings app.
  • Conversation review: switch off Improve Audel with my conversations under Account & sign-in.
  • Location, camera, microphone and notifications: change these at any time in the iPhone's Settings app.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we'll help.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as to receive a copy of your data or to object to certain processing. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

California residents

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act gives you the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and get a copy of it. This policy describes the categories: identifiers (name, email, account ID, IP address, push token), financial information (bank and investment data, subscription status), health information you connect, the content you create, and usage and diagnostic information. We collect them from you, your iPhone, and the services you connect, for the purposes described above.
  • Delete your personal information. You can delete your account in the app at any time, or ask us to.
  • Correct personal information that's inaccurate.
  • Not be discriminated against for using any of these rights. We won't charge you a different price or give you a different service because you did.

We do not sell or share personal information, as the law defines those words, and haven't in the past 12 months. We use sensitive personal information, such as financial and health information, only to provide the service you asked for, so there is nothing to limit.

To make a request, email support@audel.ai from the address on your Audel account, or use Delete account in the app. We'll confirm the request comes from you, by matching it to your account, before acting on it, and answer within 45 days. You can have an authorized agent make a request for you; we'll ask for proof that you gave them permission.

Do Not Track

Audel doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites, so there is nothing for a browser's Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal to switch off. We treat every visitor as if one were on.

Children

Audel is not intended for anyone under 18, because it connects to financial accounts. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know in the app before they take effect.
