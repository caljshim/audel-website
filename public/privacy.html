Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

The short version

  • Audel uses your data to run the app for you. It doesn't sell it, use it for targeted advertising, use it to train AI models, or track you across other apps.
  • Bank connections are read-only. Audel can't move money.
  • Before the assistant first answers you, the app asks you to agree to your messages, and the data it needs to answer them, going to the AI providers it names.
  • We review conversations with the assistant, without your name or email, to find and fix its mistakes. Health data is left out of those copies. You can turn this off in the app.
  • Health data has its own policy: our consumer health data privacy policy.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

Audel is an iPhone app for tracking goals, routines and personal finances, operated by Caleb Shim ("we", "us"), an individual in California. This policy explains what we collect when you use Audel, why, who helps us process it, and the choices and rights you have. Questions go to support@audel.ai.

What we collect

Your account

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address and, from Google, your profile photo. We use these to identify your account and show your profile. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address instead of your real one. If you sign in with Apple, we also keep a token Apple gives us, stored encrypted, so that deleting your account can end Audel's access to your Apple sign-in too. When you agree to our terms at sign-in, we record which version you agreed to and when.

What you put into Audel

Your goals, routines, reminders, calendar events, check-ins, notes and progress logs, and the times you check things off. Audel uses the times you usually complete a routine to schedule reminders.

What the assistant remembers

The assistant can remember things you tell it that will matter later, such as a preference or a lasting fact about your plans, so it doesn't have to ask again. You can see, edit and delete what it remembers, or turn memory off, in the app's Settings under What Audel remembers.

Bank and card accounts

If you connect a bank, we use Plaid to read your account names, balances and transactions, and, for investment and retirement accounts, your holdings and investment transactions. Your bank login goes to Plaid and your bank, never to us. Plaid gives us an access token for each connection, which we store encrypted. Access is read-only: Audel can see your accounts but can't make payments, transfers or trades. Plaid's handling of your data is covered by its own privacy policy.

Payments

If you subscribe, you pay Apple through the App Store, with the payment method on your Apple Account. Apple processes the payment; your card details and billing address go to Apple, never to us. Apple tells us the subscription's status: which plan, whether it's active or renewing, and until when, with the IDs Apple gives the purchase. We store those, and a random ID the app attaches to the purchase so it belongs to your Audel account, so the app knows whether it's open to you. Apple's handling of your payment is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Apple Health and other health goals

Audel reads from Apple Health only for a goal you connect to it, one kind of data at a time, after showing you a Health data notice and then Apple's own permission screen. The kinds of data Audel can read are steps, exercise minutes, active energy, workouts, walking and running, cycling, swimming, wheelchair and downhill snow sports distances, sleep, mindful minutes, mood (State of Mind), resting heart rate, heart rate variability, weight, time in daylight, and nutrition: calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber, sugar, water and alcoholic drinks. Audel only reads Health data; it never writes to Apple Health. It stores the values a goal needs to show your progress.

Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never included in the copies we review to improve Audel. It goes to an AI provider only when you ask the assistant about it. Our consumer health data privacy policy describes exactly what we collect, why, who it's shared with, and your rights over it.

Location

If you set up a goal that counts visits to a place, such as the gym, your iPhone watches for arrivals at the place you choose. The place's coordinates stay on your iPhone, stored encrypted in its Keychain; we don't receive them. When you arrive, Audel sends us only that you arrived, and when. Places that provide health care, such as hospitals and pharmacies, can't be chosen.

Photos, camera and voice

Photos you attach to a chat with the assistant are uploaded and stored with your account so the assistant can read them, and are sent to the AI providers to answer you. Before a photo is stored, we remove its metadata, including the location it was taken. Voice input is transcribed on your iPhone; we receive the text, not the audio.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, your iPhone gives us a push token, which we store with your account and use only to send you Audel's notifications through Apple's push notification service.

Usage needed to run the service

We record how much of the assistant you use, to apply usage limits, and keep server logs for security and troubleshooting. Server logs include technical details such as your IP address, request times, and errors and crash reports the app sends us. When something breaks, an alert with the error's details, but not the content of your messages, is emailed to us. Audel does not include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

How the assistant works

The first time you open the assistant, the app tells you what is sent where, naming Google (Gemini) and Anthropic (Claude), whose models write the replies, Brave, for web searches, and TypeSafe, which helps sort transactions and read your answers. It asks you to agree, and we record when you agreed and which wording you agreed to. Until you do, the assistant doesn't answer and Audel doesn't send your data to any AI provider. You can take your agreement back at any time under Account & sign-in.

When you message the assistant, your message, any photo you attach, and the relevant parts of your Audel data (for example, the goals, transactions, balances or Health values it needs to answer) are sent to the AI provider that writes the reply: Google (Gemini) or Anthropic (Claude). We use their paid APIs, under their commercial API terms, which don't allow training on your data. When the assistant looks something up on the web, the search terms are sent to Brave Search. We keep your chat history so you can see past conversations.

TypeSafe receives:

  • a new transaction's merchant, amount, date and bank category, with the names of your budgets and examples of what you've put in each (kinds of spending and merchant names), to suggest which budget it belongs in;
  • your reply when Audel asks you to confirm an action, with a short summary of that action, to tell whether you said yes or no;
  • payments you received, such as through Zelle or Venmo (who sent it, the amount, the date and the bank's description, and how you filed earlier payments from that person), with charges they might be paying you back for (merchant, amount, date and bank category), to suggest a match.

TypeSafe sends back a choice, such as a budget or a yes or no, not text.

When you look up a packaged food by barcode or name, the barcode or search words are sent from our servers to Open Food Facts. Nothing that identifies you is sent with them.

Improving Audel

To find where the assistant got something wrong and fix it, we review conversations with it. This is on unless you turn it off, and you can turn it off at any time: open your profile, tap Account & sign-in, and switch off Improve Audel with my conversations. Your chat history is still kept so you can see it.

Each night, the previous day's conversations of people who haven't turned it off are copied, without your name or email, under a coded label instead. The label is a keyed code that stays the same from day to day, so we can follow a conversation across days, and only we can link it back to an account. The copies go to private storage in Google Cloud and to our encrypted review computer, where we read them with the help of Anthropic's Claude, through Anthropic's commercial API, which doesn't allow training on your data. We use them to find mistakes and improve Audel, and may turn a mistake into a test case built from made-up example data, never your own.

Anything that came from Apple Health or another health goal, such as sleep, mood, weight, heart rate or nutrition, is left out of these copies. Photos are never included. Message text can still include other things the assistant used to answer, such as amounts. The copies are deleted within 30 days, including after you turn review off or delete your account.

Who processes your data

We share data only with the service providers that run Audel for us, and only what each needs:

  • Google Cloud, which hosts our servers and database in the United States
  • Plaid, for bank and investment connections
  • Google and Anthropic, for the assistant's replies, and Anthropic to help review conversations (see Improving Audel)
  • Brave, for web searches the assistant makes
  • TypeSafe, for sorting transactions, matching payments you received, and reading your answers to confirmations
  • Open Food Facts, for food barcode and name lookups
  • Google and Apple, for signing in, and Apple, for subscription payments and delivering notifications
  • Vercel, which hosts our website (not the app or its data)

We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Financial information notice

Audel collects financial information from you (such as the budgets and goals you set) and, when you connect a bank, from Plaid (your account names, balances, transactions and investment holdings). We share it only with the service providers that run Audel for us, as listed above, and when the law requires it. We don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing. Because we don't share it in those ways, there is nothing you need to opt out of, and you're never asked to opt in to anything like that to use Audel.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Our database accepts connections only through Google's authenticated, encrypted Cloud SQL connector, and each person's data is isolated from everyone else's in the database itself. Our servers sign in to it without a stored password. Bank access tokens and Apple sign-in tokens are encrypted with a separate key.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. If your account goes 30 days without access (no subscription after the free month, or after a subscription ends), we remove its bank connections at Plaid; data already imported is kept for as long as you have an account. If an account has no activity for 24 months and no active subscription, we delete it, after telling you at least 30 days before, in the app and with a notification.

When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database immediately, your bank connections are revoked at Plaid, and Audel's access to your Apple sign-in is revoked at Apple. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription, because Apple manages it: cancel it in your iPhone's Settings (tap your name, then Subscriptions) so you aren't charged again. Apple keeps records of your purchases under its own policy.

Copies in our encrypted backups expire within 7 days. Server logs and error reports are kept for 30 days in Google Cloud. Conversation copies made for review are deleted within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, open your profile, tap Account & sign-in, then Delete account. Or email us.
  • The assistant and AI providers: switch off Let Audel use its AI providers under Account & sign-in. The assistant stops answering and nothing more is sent to them.
  • What the assistant remembers: see, edit or delete memories under What Audel remembers in Settings, or switch off Remember things about me.
  • Disconnect a bank: in the app, open your profile, tap Connected accounts, press and hold one of the bank's accounts, then tap Disconnect. This removes the connection at Plaid and deletes that bank's accounts and transactions from Audel.
  • Apple Health: turn off Audel's access in the Health app (tap your picture, then Apps, then Audel) or in your iPhone's Settings, or remove the goal that uses it.
  • Conversation review: switch off Improve Audel with my conversations under Account & sign-in.
  • Location, camera, microphone and notifications: change these at any time in the iPhone's Settings app.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal data we have about you, and get a copy of it in a portable format;
  • Correct data that's inaccurate;
  • Delete your data (you can also delete your account in the app);
  • Withdraw consent you've given, such as to the assistant's AI providers or to Apple Health access;
  • Opt out of conversation review.

Some state laws, including those of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon and others, give these rights by law, along with the right to opt out of the sale of personal data, targeted advertising, and profiling. We don't sell personal data, use it for targeted advertising, or use it to train AI models (ours or our providers'), and we don't use automated profiling to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you, so there's nothing to opt out of.

To make a request, email support@audel.ai from the address on your Audel account. We'll confirm the request comes from you, by matching it to your account, before acting on it, and answer within 45 days. If we need more time, we'll tell you why within those 45 days. Using these rights never changes your price or your service. If we decline a request, reply to our decision to appeal; we'll answer within 60 days, and you can contact your state Attorney General if you disagree. Requests about health data have their own timelines in our consumer health data privacy policy.

To make a request, email support@audel.ai from the address on your Audel account, or use Delete account in the app. We'll confirm the request comes from you before acting on it, and answer within 45 days. If Audel changes hands

If Audel is transferred to a successor, including a company we form, or involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may transfer to it as part of that, and this policy will continue to apply to it. We'll tell you in the app before a materially different policy applies.

Our website

This website, audel.ai, is hosted by Vercel, which sees visitors' IP addresses, as any web host does. The website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and serves its fonts itself, so visiting it sends nothing to anyone else.

Do Not Track

Audel doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites, so there is nothing for a browser's Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal to switch off. We treat every visitor as if one were on.

Children

Audel is for people 18 and over, because it connects to financial accounts. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
